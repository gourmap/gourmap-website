Support

Need help with Gourmap? Email us at info@gourmap.app and we will get back to you within a few business days.

How do I delete my account?

Open your profile in the app, go to Settings, and choose Delete account. This removes your profile, visits, photos, video, comments, and likes. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I make my account private?

Open Settings in the app and turn on Private account. Only followers you approve will see your visits.

How do I report content or block someone?

Use the menu on a visit, comment, or profile in the app to report it. To stop someone from following you or seeing your visits, open their profile and choose Block. You can also report a user, visit, or comment here, or email us with a link or screenshot.

I forgot my password

Tap Forgot password on the login screen and we will email you a reset link.

Can I turn off notifications?

Yes. Open Settings in the app and switch notifications off. You can also do this in your phone's system settings.
