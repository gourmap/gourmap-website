Features

Gourmap is a private social network for the food places you and your people love.

A map of the places you love

Every restaurant you log becomes a pin on your map. See your own food history at a glance, or explore where the people you follow like to eat.

Log your visits

Record a visit with photos, a star rating, and a note about what you ordered. Edit or delete a visit whenever you like.

Find any restaurant

Search for restaurants with Apple Maps, right on your device, and add them to your visits in a few taps.

Save places you want to try

Add a restaurant to your Wants list so you remember where to eat next, whether a friend recommended it or you spotted it on the map.

Follow your people

Follow friends and see their visits in your activity feed. Follow requests need approval, so you decide who sees what you eat.

Likes and comments

React to a friend's visit with a like or start a conversation in the comments.

Notifications

Get notified about new followers, follow requests, likes, and comments. Tap a notification to jump straight to the visit.

Find people

Search for friends by name and discover people you might know.

Private by design

Only you and people you approve can see your visits. You can block and report users, and remove followers at any time.

Simple sign in, no passwords

Sign in with Apple, with Google, or with a one-time code sent to your email.

Your data is yours

Download all of your data or delete your account from Settings whenever you want.
