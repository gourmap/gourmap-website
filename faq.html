Frequently asked questions

What is Gourmap?

Gourmap is a private social network for restaurants. Log the places you eat, keep a map of your favorites, and see where your friends eat.

Is Gourmap free?

The app is free to download and use.

When can I get the app?

Gourmap is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. The Get the app button will link there once it is available.

Is there an Android version?

Not at the moment. Gourmap is currently available for iPhone only.

How do I sign in?

Gourmap has no passwords. Sign in with Apple, with Google, or with a one-time code that we email to you. If the code does not arrive, check your spam folder or request a new one.

Who can see my visits?

Only you, the people whose follow request you have approved, and friends you tag in a visit. To remove a follower, open your followers list on your profile and use the "..." menu next to them.

How do I add a restaurant?

Start a new visit and search for the restaurant by name. Search uses Apple Maps on your device. Then add photos, a rating, and a note, and save.

Can I edit or delete a visit?

Yes. Open the visit and use the menu to edit or delete it.

What is the Wants list?

It is your list of restaurants you want to try. Add a place to your Wants from its restaurant page and find it again later from your profile.

How do I follow someone?

Search for them in the Search tab and tap Follow. They will get a request to approve, and you will see their visits once they accept.

How do I report content or block someone?

Use the menu on a visit, comment, or profile in the app to report it. To stop someone from following you or seeing your visits, open their profile and choose Block. You can also report a user, visit, or comment here.

How do I download my data?

Open Settings in the app, go to Account, and choose Download All Data.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in the app, go to Account, and choose Delete My Account. This removes your profile, visits, photos, comments, likes, wanted restaurants, and follows. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I contact you?

Email us at info@gourmap.app or visit the Support page. We reply within a few business days.
